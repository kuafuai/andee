# v0.9.1

**The folded ball stops sitting on your screen and goes into the edge.**

Folded, the ball was a 136 dp square window — and *every pixel of that square was ours*. A touch that
lands in one of our windows cannot be handed to the app underneath, so the transparent band around the
sphere was dead space that travelled with the ball and swallowed taps meant for whatever was behind it.

Left alone, it now slides into the right edge and clings there, in a window a tenth the size.

> **These binaries carry no credentials.** No CodeFlying key, no voice-provider key, and no Google key.
> **You supply your own**: sign up from the link inside the app, then paste the key into
> **Settings → Backend**. Starting is free and the free allowance is finite.
>
> Each package already points at the right server for its language — the English ones at
> `myapp.codeflying.app`, the Chinese at `baas.kuafuai.net`. You do not choose this.
>
> **The macOS packages are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID**, so Gatekeeper
> refuses the first launch. On macOS 15 the old "right-click → Open" trick no longer exists; see
> **[Opening the Mac app](#opening-the-mac-app)**.

## Highlights

- **The ball goes into the edge instead of parking on your screen.** Left alone for 10 seconds, it slides
  into the right edge and clings there — paws on the edge, leaning out around it with both eyes on your
  side — leaving a window about **31 × 50 dp** instead of a 136 dp square.
- **Everything still works while it is docked**: tap talks, double-tap types, long-press opens the card.
  Drag *along* the edge to move it up and down; pull *away* to bring the whole ball out.
- **A fresh install now opens on the conversation**, not on an empty stage board.

## What's Changed

### Changed

- **The folded ball docks into the right edge.** Left alone for 10 seconds — or when a task starts, a page
  goes up, or the ball is dropped against the right edge — it slides into the right edge, shrinks, and
  clings to it. The window shrinks to just that, and excludes the back gesture.
  - **The folded ball is smaller**: `136 → 104 dp`. The floor that held it at 136 was the paw gripping the
    bottom edge, which is gone.
  - **It keeps its whole vocabulary while docked.** A signboard takes it out to hold the sign and puts it
    back at the same height when the sign times out.
  - **It no longer hangs off the bottom edge.** The side cling is the old ledge cling turned a quarter —
    paws, slip and haul all come with it, rather than being a second pose that could drift from the first.
    A first cut put the sphere's centre *on* the edge and showed half a face, which read as broken rather
    than as peeking; the body is now raised past the line so both eyes stay on your side.
  - The ball area keeps the square's size and is *translated* inside the tab, so the GL surface never
    resizes and the swap from square to tab is invisible.

### Fixed

- **The docked tab is tall enough for the rabbit's ears.** The tab was sized off Andee's horns and centred
  on the ball, so a rabbit docked into the edge had its ears cut off flat. Every look's clinging pose was
  measured in `BallLab` first: the rabbit's ears reach ~0.38 of the side above the centre against Andee's
  horns at ~0.27, and every paw ~0.29 below. The tab now reaches **0.44 above and 0.34 below**.
  Verified on the HONOR phone: a signboard raised while docked brings the ball out to hold it, and the
  ball goes back in at the same height.
- **A new device opens on the conversation until the stage has something on it.** The card's middle
  defaulted to the stage, so a fresh install landed on an empty board ("not furnished yet") and the
  conversation's empty state — the scene bubbles that are a new user's way in — was hidden behind a tab.
  Until you pick 舞台 or 对话, the middle follows the stage: the conversation while it is empty, the stage
  from its first write on, and back to the conversation if a wipe empties it. **A pick, once made, is kept
  whatever the stage holds.**
  Verified on the HONOR phone with the pick cleared and the stage moved aside.

### Internal

- `CLAUDE.md` records the dock: the constants, why the side cling is a roll of the ledge rather than a
  second pose, and that `BallLab --ez side true` draws it. The folded size a face has to survive is now
  **`dp 104`** (it was 168 until 2026-10-23, then 136 until today).

### Opening the Mac app

The `.dmg`s are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper refuses the first
launch of a downloaded copy.

⚠️ **On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, "right-click → Open" no longer works** — Apple removed that
override, and most advice online still recommends it. The current route:

1. Double-click the app. It will be refused. Dismiss the dialog.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll to **Security**.
3. There is a line naming Andee as blocked, with an **Open Anyway** button beside it.
4. Confirm, and authenticate.

That button **only appears for about an hour** after the failed launch. If it is missing, launch the app
again and go straight back to Settings.

One more thing worth knowing: the app **inside** the mounted dmg carries no quarantine flag, so it may
launch straight from the dmg — and then be blocked once you drag it to Applications, which does attach
one. Both behaviours are normal; neither means a damaged download.

### What you supply

No package has a key. After installing, open **Settings → Backend** and either paste a CodeFlying key
(sign up from the link in that panel) or switch to **on-device** and give it an OpenAI-compatible
endpoint and model.

**For voice, a key alone may not be enough.** If you use Volcengine, the service must be an *entitled*
resource, not just an opened one — see
[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md). Android can also use the system voice instead, and
without any voice it is silent but still works on screen.

> **Would you rather not configure keys at all?** Use the one-click page instead —
> **[codeflying.app/andee](https://www.codeflying.app/andee)**, or
> **[codeflying.net/andee](https://www.codeflying.net/andee)** in mainland China. Describe what you want,
> tap the build button, and you get a package with the **tokens already configured** plus a QR code to
> scan. Starting is free and the free token allowance is **finite — once it runs out, tokens must be
> bought.** It is a separate service with no direct relationship to this project, with its own terms,
> pricing and data handling. Note it produces **an Android APK, not a Mac app** — macOS packages can
> only be built on macOS.

## Known issues

- **The ball's face has never been verified on real hardware here.** This release moves the folded size
  (`136 → 104 dp`) and changes how the ball sits when folded, and both were checked against the offscreen
  renderer and the phone — but the underlying *face* is the same one introduced in v0.8.1, and this
  project has never looked at it on a device. The eyes are 3D capsules under perspective projection, so a
  flat rendering cannot stand in for the real thing, and measuring a still image has been wrong every time
  it was tried. Look at it on a device before assuming it is right.
- **The dock's height was measured in `BallLab`, not on every device.** Every look's clinging pose was
  measured so the tab clears the rabbit's ears (the tallest thing any look puts above its centre), and
  verified on one phone. A device that scales or insets the overlay differently could still clip.
- **What it keeps for itself is per device.** The one on your phone and the one on your Mac each have
  their own record — their own self-description, their own cares, their own people. They do not share a
  notebook. This is deliberate rather than pending: one individual does not live in two bodies, and it
  avoids cross-device sync, conflicts and doubled budgets as a class of problem.
- **Nothing verifies that a key is accepted by the server it is sent to.** These packages are checked for
  the right server and the absence of credentials; whether a given CodeFlying key works against
  `baas.kuafuai.net` versus `myapp.codeflying.app` has not been exercised end-to-end.
- **The Mac still falls back silently when no server is configured.** Every package here carries one, so it
  does not arise for these downloads — but a locally built app with an empty domain resolves to the local
  backend and sends whatever key it has to `api.deepseek.com`, while the self-check reports "Configured".
- **`docs/design/inner-drive.zh-CN.md` still opens with "草案，未实现"** although the classes it describes
  are all present. That status line is the author's to change.
- **The wake threshold's margin is thin**, carried from `v0.3.2`: the eight measured judgements sit in
  1.47–2.05 with the line landing in the gap between 1.69 and 1.77 — 0.08 wide.
- **A light generated page still fades in over a dark stage.** Carried from `v0.3.1`; the app's own
  default has been light since `v0.4.1`.
- **`assets/andee_key.txt` is tracked by git and is not in `.gitignore`.** It ships with a single
  newline, which reads as empty. A key written into it would outrank every build setting.
- Two issues from `v0.1.0` are **unchanged and untouched**: `tools/verify_faces_lab.py` still reports
  7/16 assertions passing, and the debug WebSocket server (`net/BodyWsServer`) still binds
  `0.0.0.0:9008` with no authentication (documented in `SECURITY.md`).

## Before you install

Read **[DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md)** and
**[docs/acceptable-use.md](../acceptable-use.md)** first. On Android the app asks for accessibility,
overlay, notification access, SMS and call log, and it can complete a payment by tapping; on the Mac it
asks for accessibility, screen recording and notifications. The only guardrail on that path is Android's
`FLAG_SECURE`; the prompt-level checks on risky actions are instructions to the model, not enforcement.

**It keeps its own record now, and that record is on your device.** The notebook is encrypted with a
key in the platform keystore; the wiki is plain files by design. Anything you would not want in a plain
file should stay in the notebook side.

## Install from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/kuafuai/andee
cd andee
git checkout v0.9.1

# one variant, by hand:
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug -PdefaultLang=en -PcodeflyingDomain=myapp.codeflying.app -PcodeflyingSsl=true

# or all ten files, the way the release was built:
bash ~/.workbuddy/skills/andee-release-ship/scripts/build_variants.sh
```

`-PcodeflyingKey=` and `-PgoogleVoiceKey=` are refused on purpose — a published package carries no
credential.

There is a getting-started guide: **[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md)**, and
**[docs/getting-started.zh-CN.md](../getting-started.zh-CN.md)** in Chinese.

**Upgrading:** `versionCode` moves `20 → 21`, so the Android package installs over v0.9.0 normally. The
Mac app's version string follows the same number. Existing memory, notebook, todos and chat history are
untouched; if you had picked one of the other faces, your choice is unaffected — only the default face
changed. **Nothing changes on your behalf this time.** The ball's new behaviour is the same ball; if you had
pinned it somewhere or were used to it hanging off the bottom edge, it will now go into the right edge
when you leave it alone for ten seconds.
