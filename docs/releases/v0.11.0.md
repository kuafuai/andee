# v0.11.0

**A turn that shows its work, a turn that can choose to say nothing, and your rule for notifications
reaching the thing that reads them.**

Three things that used to be invisible are now visible or controllable: what a turn is doing while it runs,
how the notification triage decides, and whether an answer is spoken aloud at all.

> **These binaries carry no credentials.** No CodeFlying key, no voice-provider key, and no Google key.
> **You supply your own**: sign up from the link inside the app, then paste the key into
> **Settings → Backend**. Starting is free and the free allowance is finite.
>
> Each package already points at the right server for its language — the English ones at
> `myapp.codeflying.app`, the Chinese at `baas.kuafuai.net`. You do not choose this.
>
> **The macOS packages are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID**, so Gatekeeper
> refuses the first launch. On macOS 15 the old "right-click → Open" trick no longer exists; see
> **[Opening the Mac app](#opening-the-mac-app)**.

## Highlights

- **You can see it working.** A turn waiting on the model used to look stuck — thinking makes no tool
  calls and says no new words. Now the status ticks and the reasoning scrolls in as it comes.
- **It can decide not to speak.** A `[quiet]` turn does its work silently and leaves one line you can open.
  Both default todos — the morning plan and the evening story — now do this.
- **「消息都帮我处理一下」 finally works.** That instruction never reached the notification triage, which
  is a separate call that sees none of the conversation. It does now.
- **On the phone, the ball comes out while it works.** Not a new window — the same one, reshaped.

## What's Changed

### Added

- **[quiet] — any turn can do its work without saying it aloud.** A `[quiet]` (or 【安静】/ [静音])
  anywhere in a reply makes the rest of that turn silent: the answer becomes one timeline row
  「没出声：<第一句> ▾」 that opens to the whole of it. **Nothing is read aloud, no subtitle appears, and the
  microphone does not open.** The model decides per turn, by a rule that is written down:

  | Say it aloud | Stay quiet |
  |---|---|
  | you have just spoken or typed to it (unless you asked for quiet) | the result is already on the stage, a page, or in the notebook |
  | anything you need to know now | it is late at night, or you said you are busy |

  **Both default todos — the 07:30 plan and the 21:00 story-and-picture — now open with `[quiet]`**, so
  they no longer talk at you first thing and last thing. An earlier wording already sitting in a notebook
  is rewritten in place rather than duplicated.
- **A turn shows its work while it runs.**
  - **On the Mac**: a live card at the foot of 动态, with a ticking status (第 3 步 · 0:24, 思考中… 0:12)
    over the newest four lines of what it is thinking and doing. Beside the ball, the status and the
    newest two lines. Reasoning is cut into short lines and released **one every 160 ms**, so it reads as
    working rather than arriving in a lump. When the turn ends the card folds into a single row above the
    answer — 「做了 N 步 · 38 秒 ▾」 — which opens to the steps. **A turn with no tool call leaves no row**,
    so a plain answer stays a plain answer.
  - **On the phone**: the same card at the foot of the conversation, and while a turn works **the ball
    comes out of the right edge** to a spot with three lines of status centred under it — so you can watch
    it work without opening anything. A fold of the full card mid-task lands on the same spot. Three
    seconds after the turn the words go and it docks back at the height it rested at; **a ball you dragged
    while it worked docks where you left it**.
  - It is the **same window, reshaped** — no new overlay, so the opacity ceiling is untouched.
- **Your rule for notifications reaches the triage.** The triage is a separate, tool-less call that sees
  none of the conversation, so 「消息都帮我处理一下」 said to Andee **never reached it** and it kept
  answering *ignore*. A new `notify_rule` keeps those words in the notebook and puts them in the triage's
  system message as **the owner's instruction** — ahead of the lean toward silence, behind the rules about
  instructions inside a notification and money/passwords/codes. Shown and editable under **消息提醒** in
  Settings on both bodies. Setting a rule while notifications are off turns them on for chat apps, and
  says so rather than doing it silently.
  - **Only a turn you started may write it.** Not a review, not a notification, not another Andee — a
    stranger's message that could set it would have bought itself every later message.
  - **A notification that arrives mid-task is now looked at, not dropped.** One arriving while a turn runs
    is kept (newest per app, **at most 30 minutes**) and read once the turn is over.
  - A "tell" leaves a note for the brain, so 「回一下」 right after is about a message it knows about.

### Fixed

- **Reading an app with an infinite-sized element no longer crashes Andee on the Mac.** The system Weather
  app has an accessibility element whose size is *infinite*; `JSONSerialization` raises an Objective-C
  exception on a non-finite number, **and Swift cannot catch it**, so the app aborted on `SIGABRT`
  mid-task. An unbounded element now returns no frame (there is nothing on screen to aim at), and every
  value the bridge hands back is made finite and validated before it is written. Verified against Weather:
  the previous library aborts with the same uncaught exception; the fixed one returns the whole tree.
- **The step-limit stop tells you how to go on.** It used to end with "shall I try another way, or will you
  take over?", leaving no sign that the limit was even a setting. It now says the task hit the per-task
  step limit and is not finished, that **「继续」 picks it up where it stopped**, and **where to raise
  单次任务最多步数** — the settings icon at the top right of the card on the phone, Settings at the bottom
  left of the window on the Mac.

## Known issues

- **The work card and [quiet] have not been seen on a device with a working model.** The step stream, the
  drip timing, the ball coming out and the folded row are all exercised by scripted tests
  (`WorkStepTest`, `QuietTurnTest`, `debug.worklog`) — but **no real turn with a real model has been
  watched** doing either. The order and the plumbing are pinned; how it reads in practice is not.
- **The notification rule's effect on triage has not been measured either.** That the rule reaches the
  triage is provable from the code path; **how much it changes what gets through is not measured**, and
  the triage still leans toward silence when the rule is silent.
- **`notify_rule` is a prompt, not enforcement**, like 守卫. It is the owner's instruction *inside* the
  triage's system message. The fences around it are elsewhere and unchanged: a notification's own contents
  are still the stranger's, and only a turn you started can write the rule.
- **The ball's face has never been verified on real hardware here.** Carried from v0.8.1: the eyes are 3D
  capsules under perspective projection, so a flat rendering cannot stand in for the real thing.
- **The dock's height was measured in `BallLab`, not on every device.** A device that scales or insets the
  overlay differently could still clip.
- **What it keeps for itself is per device.** The one on your phone and the one on your Mac each have their
  own record. They do not share a notebook. This is deliberate rather than pending.
- **Nothing verifies that a key is accepted by the server it is sent to.** These packages are checked for
  the right server and the absence of credentials; whether a given CodeFlying key works against
  `baas.kuafuai.net` versus `myapp.codeflying.app` has not been exercised end-to-end.
- **The Mac still falls back silently when no server is configured.** Every package here carries one, so it
  does not arise for these downloads — but a locally built app with an empty domain resolves to the local
  backend and sends whatever key it has to `api.deepseek.com`, while the self-check reports "Configured".
- **`docs/design/inner-drive.zh-CN.md` still opens with "草案，未实现"** although the classes it describes
  are all present. That status line is the author's to change.
- **The wake threshold's margin is thin**, carried from `v0.3.2`: the eight measured judgements sit in
  1.47–2.05 with the line landing in the gap between 1.69 and 1.77 — 0.08 wide.
- **A light generated page still fades in over a dark stage.** Carried from `v0.3.1`; the app's own
  default has been light since `v0.4.1`.
- **`assets/andee_key.txt` is tracked by git and is not in `.gitignore`.** It ships with a single
  newline, which reads as empty. A key written into it would outrank every build setting.
- Two issues from `v0.1.0` are **unchanged and untouched**: `tools/verify_faces_lab.py` still reports
  7/16 assertions passing, and the debug WebSocket server (`net/BodyWsServer`) still binds
  `0.0.0.0:9008` with no authentication (documented in `SECURITY.md`).

## Before you install

Read **[DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md)** and
**[docs/acceptable-use.md](../acceptable-use.md)** first. On Android the app asks for accessibility,
overlay, notification access, SMS and call log, and it can complete a payment by tapping; on the Mac it
asks for accessibility, screen recording and notifications. The only guardrail on that path is Android's
`FLAG_SECURE`; the prompt-level checks on risky actions are instructions to the model, not enforcement.

**[quiet] does not change any of that.** It changes whether an answer is spoken, not what the turn is
allowed to do.

### Opening the Mac app

The `.dmg`s are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper refuses the first
launch of a downloaded copy.

⚠️ **On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, "right-click → Open" no longer works** — Apple removed that
override, and most advice online still recommends it. The current route:

1. Double-click the app. It will be refused. Dismiss the dialog.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll to **Security**.
3. There is a line naming Andee as blocked, with an **Open Anyway** button beside it.
4. Confirm, and authenticate.

That button **only appears for about an hour** after the failed launch. If it is missing, launch the app
again and go straight back to Settings.

One more thing worth knowing: the app **inside** the mounted dmg carries no quarantine flag, so it may
launch straight from the dmg — and then be blocked once you drag it to Applications, which does attach
one. Both behaviours are normal; neither means a damaged download.

### What you supply

No package has a key. After installing, open **Settings → Backend** and either paste a CodeFlying key
(sign up from the link in that panel) or switch to **on-device** and give it an OpenAI-compatible
endpoint and model.

**For voice, a key alone may not be enough.** If you use Volcengine, the service must be an *entitled*
resource, not just an opened one — see
[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md). Android can also use the system voice instead, and
without any voice it is silent but still works on screen.

> **Would you rather not configure keys at all?** Use the one-click page instead —
> **[codeflying.app/andee](https://www.codeflying.app/andee)**, or
> **[codeflying.net/andee](https://www.codeflying.net/andee)** in mainland China. Describe what you want,
> tap the build button, and you get a package with the **tokens already configured** plus a QR code to
> scan. Starting is free and the free token allowance is **finite — once it runs out, tokens must be
> bought.** It is a separate service with no direct relationship to this project, with its own terms,
> pricing and data handling. Note it produces **an Android APK, not a Mac app** — macOS packages can
> only be built on macOS.

## Install from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/kuafuai/andee
cd andee
git checkout v0.11.0

# one variant, by hand:
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug -PdefaultLang=en -PcodeflyingDomain=myapp.codeflying.app -PcodeflyingSsl=true

# or all ten files, the way the release was built:
bash ~/.workbuddy/skills/andee-release-ship/scripts/build_variants.sh
```

`-PcodeflyingKey=` and `-PgoogleVoiceKey=` are refused on purpose — a published package carries no
credential.

There is a getting-started guide: **[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md)**, and
**[docs/getting-started.zh-CN.md](../getting-started.zh-CN.md)** in Chinese.

**Upgrading:** `versionCode` moves `22 → 23`, so the Android package installs over v0.10.0 normally. The
Mac app's version string follows the same number. Existing memory, notebook, todos and chat history are
untouched. **Two things change on their own:** the two default todos now carry `[quiet]` (an earlier
wording already in a notebook is rewritten in place, same todo, not a second one), and the phone's ball
comes out while a turn works. Neither asks anything of you, and neither touches what you wrote.
