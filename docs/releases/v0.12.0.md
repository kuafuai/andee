# v0.12.0

**桌宠 — Andee gets a second body, and it is a character rather than a ball.**

A small figure that lives on the desktop and on the phone, wearing one of five looks, each of which is a
character with its own voice and manner. On the Mac it **replaces** the floating ball rather than standing
beside it; on the phone it draws inside the ball's own window. Both follow the same rules, because what
they share lives in `core`.

> **These binaries carry no credentials.** No CodeFlying key, no voice-provider key, and no Google key.
> **You supply your own**: sign up from the link inside the app, then paste the key into
> **Settings → Backend**. Starting is free and the free allowance is finite.
>
> Each package already points at the right server for its language — the English ones at
> `myapp.codeflying.app`, the Chinese at `baas.kuafuai.net`. You do not choose this.
>
> **The macOS packages are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID**, so Gatekeeper
> refuses the first launch. On macOS 15 the old "right-click → Open" trick no longer exists; see
> **[Opening the Mac app](#opening-the-mac-app)**.

## Highlights

- **A body that stands on your desktop.** She sits in one window, falls with gravity, hops when you pat
  her, says a line above her head, and stands wherever you put her — not glued to the bottom of the screen.
- **Five characters, each with its own voice.** Andee itself (the plush ball of the brand illustrations),
  奶油金, 奶油金·短发, 大肥鱼, 小猫. Picking one gives it a voice and a manner, on both bodies.
- **The same rules on both bodies**, because she runs on the phone too — in the ball's own window, so
  nothing new is added to the screen.
- **She naps, she does not fall asleep for good.** Quiet for three minutes and she dozes; 40–80 seconds
  later she wakes on her own and starts pottering about again.
- **A page has a proper title bar now**, instead of a ✕完成 pill floating over its top-right corner.

## What's Changed

### Added

- **A desktop pet (Mac).** One transparent window the size of her bounding box: no dock icon, no focus
  stealing, gravity, a hop when you pat her, a line of dialogue above her head, and a look library you can
  extend with your own picture.
  - **The window *is* her bounding box**, so the speech bubble is drawn inside it and shares one coordinate
    space with the body. The first version gave the bubble its own window and it drifted away from her at
    screen edges — one window, one owner of the geometry.
  - **Input is read from the physical mouse**, not from the UI toolkit's pointer events: once she starts
    following the pointer the window moves too, macOS retargets the pointer, and the event stream carrying
    the drag simply ends. A press reacts without moving her — **the hop belongs to the release of a click,
    not to the press of a drag.**
  - **She stands where she is put**, not on the bottom of the screen. A throw clears the perch and she falls
    to the floor; landing re-perches her.
- **桌宠 on the phone**, with the Mac's rules. She draws **in the ball's own window** — no new overlay —
  folded and unfolded alike, mirroring the sphere's scale and drop. The hit test is her pixels; she has
  idle acts, a nap she wakes from, and a hop on tap.
  - Docked into the right edge she **peeks round it**; the dock rules are the ball's, and the tab is sized
    from the drawing.
  - **Settings → 形象与表情** picks 小球 / 桌宠 and then the pet, with a preview for each expression.
    **A fresh install wears the ball** — the pet is opt-in. The swipe on the ball walks one ring across
    the ball's looks and the pets.
- **Five characters** (`PetCharacter`, in `core` so both bodies agree) — **Andee** (first preset),
  **奶油金 / Cream**, **奶油金·短发 / Cream short**, **大肥鱼 / Chubby fish**, **小猫 / Kitten**. Voice and
  persona follow the character in force on both bodies. The old cobalt pair is gone, and **a saved
  `blue-*` id reads as its successor** rather than breaking.

### Changed

- **The pet is the body, not a companion to it.** On the Mac the app shows the pet **or** the floating
  ball, never both, and the caption, the prompt card and the bubble follow whichever is the body. The pet
  takes the ball's whole vocabulary — tap talks, double-tap types, triple stops, long-press opens the
  window — and wears Andee's state: listening, speaking with the face the model wrote, searching while a
  task acts, thinking while it waits, holding while a card is up.
- **A page gets a thin glass title bar** instead of a floating ✕完成 pill. The pill sat over the page's
  top-right corner, which is exactly where a generated page puts its own things (a score, a button), and
  its look belonged to neither the page nor the rest of Andee. The bar sits **above** the page — title
  centred and ellipsised, a ✕ in a small round well — so nothing of the page is covered, and it takes the
  page's own background colour once loaded, the way Safari's toolbar does.

### Fixed

- **Only a click on her pixels is a click on her.** Because the window is her bounding box, every empty
  corner of it was answering: the hop fired on any release, and the transparent margin swallowed clicks
  meant for the app behind it. The hit test now samples the alpha of what is on screen (half resolution,
  thresholded above the antialiased edge), and the window stops ignoring the mouse only while the cursor is
  over her — **a click beside her head reaches the window underneath.**
- **She naps instead of sleeping for good.** Nothing but a drag, a task or a greeting used to clear
  *asleep*, so after three quiet minutes she slept until touched and her idle acts stopped. She now wakes by
  herself after 40–80 seconds (a yawn, then idle and the acts resume), and a click wakes her too.
- **A page going up no longer docks a full-size ball at the top right.** `show_html` folds the card and
  docks the ball in the same moment, so the dock ran mid-fold, read the card's `p.y = 0`, and docked at the
  top right; the fold's end then reset the ball to scale 1 inside the small tab — a full-size ball cut in
  half over the page. A dock asked for while the card is still folding now waits for the fold to land.
  Reproduced on the HONOR phone with the previous build (tab at 1087,152, ball at scale 1); with the fix,
  three runs each docked at 1087,2267 at the docked scale.

## Known issues

- **Neither pet has been used for a long stretch on a real desktop.** The Mac pet was built and checked
  against the drawings and the hit test, and the phone's dock/peek was verified on the HONOR phone — but
  **nobody has lived with either one for a day.** Things that only show up over time (whether she ends up
  in the way, whether the idle acts feel like company or a distraction, whether the nap timing is right)
  have not had the time to show up.
- **The pet's own picture library is Mac-only for now.** You can extend the Mac pet with your own image;
  the phone ships the five presets and cannot add a sixth yet.
- **The phone's pet adds about 6.6 MB to the APK** (a scaled copy of the drawings: 560 px stills, 520 px
  frames). That is on top of whatever the ball already cost.
- **The ball's face has never been verified on real hardware here.** Carried from v0.8.1: the eyes are 3D
  capsules under perspective projection, so a flat rendering cannot stand in for the real thing.
- **The dock's height was measured in `BallLab`, not on every device.** A device that scales or insets the
  overlay differently could still clip.
- **What it keeps for itself is per device.** The one on your phone and the one on your Mac each have their
  own record. They do not share a notebook. This is deliberate rather than pending.
- **Nothing verifies that a key is accepted by the server it is sent to.** These packages are checked for
  the right server and the absence of credentials; whether a given CodeFlying key works against
  `baas.kuafuai.net` versus `myapp.codeflying.app` has not been exercised end-to-end.
- **The Mac still falls back silently when no server is configured.** Every package here carries one, so it
  does not arise for these downloads — but a locally built app with an empty domain resolves to the local
  backend and sends whatever key it has to `api.deepseek.com`, while the self-check reports "Configured".
- **`docs/design/inner-drive.zh-CN.md` still opens with "草案，未实现"** although the classes it describes
  are all present. That status line is the author's to change.
- **The wake threshold's margin is thin**, carried from `v0.3.2`: the eight measured judgements sit in
  1.47–2.05 with the line landing in the gap between 1.69 and 1.77 — 0.08 wide.
- **A light generated page still fades in over a dark stage.** Carried from `v0.3.1`; the app's own
  default has been light since `v0.4.1`.
- **`assets/andee_key.txt` is tracked by git and is not in `.gitignore`.** It ships with a single
  newline, which reads as empty. A key written into it would outrank every build setting.
- Two issues from `v0.1.0` are **unchanged and untouched**: `tools/verify_faces_lab.py` still reports
  7/16 assertions passing, and the debug WebSocket server (`net/BodyWsServer`) still binds
  `0.0.0.0:9008` with no authentication (documented in `SECURITY.md`).

## Before you install

Read **[DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md)** and
**[docs/acceptable-use.md](../acceptable-use.md)** first. On Android the app asks for accessibility,
overlay, notification access, SMS and call log, and it can complete a payment by tapping; on the Mac it
asks for accessibility, screen recording and notifications. The only guardrail on that path is Android's
`FLAG_SECURE`; the prompt-level checks on risky actions are instructions to the model, not enforcement.

**The pet is a body, not a new permission.** It draws in the window the ball already had, and asks for
nothing the ball did not.

### Opening the Mac app

The `.dmg`s are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper refuses the first
launch of a downloaded copy.

⚠️ **On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, "right-click → Open" no longer works** — Apple removed that
override, and most advice online still recommends it. The current route:

1. Double-click the app. It will be refused. Dismiss the dialog.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll to **Security**.
3. There is a line naming Andee as blocked, with an **Open Anyway** button beside it.
4. Confirm, and authenticate.

That button **only appears for about an hour** after the failed launch. If it is missing, launch the app
again and go straight back to Settings.

One more thing worth knowing: the app **inside** the mounted dmg carries no quarantine flag, so it may
launch straight from the dmg — and then be blocked once you drag it to Applications, which does attach
one. Both behaviours are normal; neither means a damaged download.

### What you supply

No package has a key. After installing, open **Settings → Backend** and either paste a CodeFlying key
(sign up from the link in that panel) or switch to **on-device** and give it an OpenAI-compatible
endpoint and model.

**For voice, a key alone may not be enough.** If you use Volcengine, the service must be an *entitled*
resource, not just an opened one — see
[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md). Android can also use the system voice instead, and
without any voice it is silent but still works on screen.

> **Would you rather not configure keys at all?** Use the one-click page instead —
> **[codeflying.app/andee](https://www.codeflying.app/andee)**, or
> **[codeflying.net/andee](https://www.codeflying.net/andee)** in mainland China. Describe what you want,
> tap the build button, and you get a package with the **tokens already configured** plus a QR code to
> scan. Starting is free and the free token allowance is **finite — once it runs out, tokens must be
> bought.** It is a separate service with no direct relationship to this project, with its own terms,
> pricing and data handling. Note it produces **an Android APK, not a Mac app** — macOS packages can
> only be built on macOS.

## Install from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/kuafuai/andee
cd andee
git checkout v0.12.0

# one variant, by hand:
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug -PdefaultLang=en -PcodeflyingDomain=myapp.codeflying.app -PcodeflyingSsl=true

# or all ten files, the way the release was built:
bash ~/.workbuddy/skills/andee-release-ship/scripts/build_variants.sh
```

`-PcodeflyingKey=` and `-PgoogleVoiceKey=` are refused on purpose — a published package carries no
credential.

There is a getting-started guide: **[docs/getting-started.md](../getting-started.md)**, and
**[docs/getting-started.zh-CN.md](../getting-started.zh-CN.md)** in Chinese.

**Upgrading:** `versionCode` moves `23 → 24`, so the Android package installs over v0.11.0 normally. The
Mac app's version string follows the same number. Existing memory, notebook, todos and chat history are
untouched. **Nothing changes on your behalf:** a fresh install wears the ball (小球), and the pet is
something you pick in **Settings → 形象与表情** — on the Mac, picking it replaces the floating ball.
**If you had saved one of the two cobalt looks, it now reads as its successor** rather than breaking.
