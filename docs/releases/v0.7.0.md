# v0.7.0

**It can speak first now, and it has a reason to.**

Until this release, every turn began with you. The alarms, the notification triage, the nightly review —
all of them were reflexes to something that had already happened. This adds the two things that turn a
reflex into an intention: a **way** to open its mouth that costs almost nothing, and a **plan** that
gives the opening a why.

The rule behind it is that the cost of interrupting is asymmetric. Missing one chance to speak costs you
a question. One interruption too many gets the ball muted — and then the good ones are silenced with the
bad. So the default is silence, speaking needs a reason, and the reason is rationed.

> **These binaries carry no credentials.** No CodeFlying key, no voice-provider key, and no Google key.
> **You supply your own**: sign up from the link inside the app, then paste the key into
> **Settings → Backend**. Starting is free and the free allowance is finite.
>
> Each package already points at the right server for its language — the English ones at
> `myapp.codeflying.app`, the Chinese at `baas.kuafuai.net`. You do not choose this.
>
> **The macOS packages are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID**, so Gatekeeper
> refuses the first launch. On macOS 15 the old "right-click → Open" trick no longer exists; see
> **[Opening the Mac app](#opening-the-mac-app)**.

## Highlights

- **A small silent bubble** — the lightest way it can speak first. Nothing is read aloud, the microphone
  stays off, nothing blocks. If you ignore it, it fades. With buttons, a tap comes back later as its own
  turn; the button that means "no thanks" closes it **without** waking the model, because a refusal does
  not need an answer. It stays quiet while you are busy, talking, locked, or asleep (22:30–08:00), and
  **Settings → 主动招呼** is where you set how much of it you want.
- **A plan it can be held to.** Long-term directions and a day plan, both **visible and editable in
  Settings → 它的打算**. Directions may only come from what you actually said or did, and each says when
  and what — a goal the model thinks would be good for you is not yours to be given.
- **A morning look and a spaced muse.** Once a day it looks at the day ahead; for items with loose timing
  it thinks again later. The nightly review compares what actually happened before drafting tomorrow.
- **`继续` works after a long task.** A task cut off by the step limit used to lose its context, so
  `继续` started over instead of carrying on. It now resumes where it stopped.
- **The Mac can find your notifications on every macOS.** It looked in one place only; on earlier systems
  that reported *missing permission*, so granting it changed nothing. "No store anywhere" and "the store
  refuses us" are now separate states, and only the second offers a trip to System Settings.

## What's Changed

### Added

- **Bubbles (`nudge`)** — silent, non-blocking, and they yield rather than displace. A bubble is an
  attempt to be useful, not a claim on your attention, so it always gives way to a card and never takes
  its slot. It is rationed per hour, per day and per kind at a level you pick in
  **Settings → 主动招呼** — *少* is at most 1 an hour and 4 a day, *正常* is 2 and 8 — and a kind can be
  muted by long-press. It does not appear while you are busy, in a conversation, locked out, or during
  quiet hours. Tapping the bubble's text — not a button — opens an ordinary conversation, the equivalent
  of you saying "go on, then".
- **Plans (`plan_directions`, `day_plan`)** — the long-term layer and the daily layer. A timed item in a
  day plan becomes a bubble raised by the device with **no model call at all**, and its outcome is
  written back to the plan. Each body keeps its own plan; the phone and the Mac do not share one.
- **`kitten` look and voice**, and stronger personas across the existing looks.

### Fixed

- **`继续` after the step limit picks up where the task stopped.** Three faults at once: the sentence you
  heard never reached the history, so the model did not know it had been cut off; the trim protected only
  the newest turn, so a todo firing at the wrong moment made the task "old" and dropped it (measured
  2026-10-22: 104 messages trimmed to 52, task gone); and tool calls left unanswered by the stopped turn
  made the next request fail with HTTP 400. A stopped turn is now kept **unfinished** until a later turn
  finishes it or an hour passes.
- **Android: tapping the ball mid-task no longer stops listening at once.** Listening ended on every loss
  of audio focus — a rule speaking needs, wrongly applied to the side that makes no sound. Mid task, the
  app in front takes the floor with its autoplay or a message tone, closing the turn the tap had just
  opened. A duck request is now ignored, a call still ends the turn, and any other taker is asked to hand
  the floor back while listening carries on.
- **macOS: the notification store is found on every version.** The search covered only the group
  container macOS 15 uses; earlier systems keep it under the per-user Darwin directory. Not finding it
  was reported as *missing Full Disk Access*, so granting the permission and restarting changed nothing —
  a correct diagnosis pointed at the wrong problem. Each known location is now tried in turn, the two
  failure states are separate, and the read happens in-process rather than through a forked
  `/usr/bin/sqlite3`, which had been swallowing the reason a read failed.

### Notes on verification

Stated plainly, because a release note that overstates its testing is worse than one that admits a gap:

- The Android audio-focus fix is **reasoned from the code, not from a capture** — the Huawei test phone
  keeps no app logs and its voice key is invalid. It is not yet verified on a device.
- The macOS notification fix was verified on **macOS 15.5** against the live store, including a row still
  in the write-ahead log, and against a fake home directory for both failure states. The **macOS 14
  location is not verified on a device**.

### Opening the Mac app

The `.dmg`s are signed with a local identity, not an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper refuses the first
launch of a downloaded copy.

⚠️ **On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, "right-click → Open" no longer works** — Apple removed that
override, and most advice online still recommends it. The current route:

1. Double-click the app. It will be refused. Dismiss the dialog.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll to **Security**.
3. There is a line naming Andee as blocked, with an **Open Anyway** button beside it.
4. Confirm, and authenticate.

That button **only appears for about an hour** after the failed launch. If it is missing, launch the app
again and go straight back to Settings.

One more thing worth knowing: the app **inside** the mounted dmg carries no quarantine flag, so it may
launch straight from the dmg — and then be blocked once you drag it to Applications, which does attach
one. Both behaviours are normal; neither means a damaged download.

### What you supply

No package has a key. After installing, open **Settings → Backend** and either paste a CodeFlying key
(sign up from the link in that panel) or switch to **on-device** and give it an OpenAI-compatible
endpoint and model. A voice key is optional — without one the assistant is silent but still works on
screen.

> **Would you rather not configure keys at all?** Use the one-click page instead —
> **[codeflying.app/andee](https://www.codeflying.app/andee)**, or
> **[codeflying.net/andee](https://www.codeflying.net/andee)** in mainland China. Describe what you want,
> tap the build button, and you get a package with the **tokens already configured** plus a QR code to
> scan. Starting is free and the free token allowance is **finite — once it runs out, tokens must be
> bought.** It is a separate service with no direct relationship to this project, with its own terms,
> pricing and data handling. Note it produces **an Android APK, not a Mac app** — macOS packages can
> only be built on macOS.

## Known issues

- **Speaking first is rationed, and the ration is deliberately low.** If it feels too quiet, raise
  **Settings → 主动招呼**. If it feels like too much, the honest description is that the ration is working
  as designed and that setting is the dial to turn — not that the feature is broken.
- **A bubble never wakes the model to check itself.** It is written at one moment and shown at another,
  so a bubble can arrive after the thing it mentions has stopped being true. The time-to-live is what
  bounds this; it is a real limitation, not a hypothetical one.
- **Nothing verifies that a key is accepted by the server it is sent to.** These packages are checked for
  the right server and the absence of credentials; whether a given CodeFlying key works against
  `baas.kuafuai.net` versus `myapp.codeflying.app` has not been exercised end-to-end.
- **The Mac still falls back silently when no server is configured.** Every package here carries one, so
  it does not arise for these downloads — but a locally built app with an empty domain resolves to the
  local backend and sends whatever key it has to `api.deepseek.com`, while the self-check reports
  "Configured".
- **A Mac build made on another machine without its signing keychain is ad-hoc signed** — its identity
  locks to the build hash, so installing it over an existing copy makes macOS treat it as a **different
  app**: Accessibility and Screen Recording must be granted again. User data is unaffected; it lives in
  `~/Library/Application Support/Andee`.
- **The wake threshold's margin is thin**, carried from `v0.3.2`: the eight measured judgements sit in
  1.47–2.05 with the line landing in the gap between 1.69 and 1.77 — 0.08 wide.
- **A light generated page still fades in over a dark stage.** Carried from `v0.3.1`; the app's own
  default has been light since `v0.4.1`.
- **`assets/andee_key.txt` is tracked by git and is not in `.gitignore`.** It ships with a single
  newline, which reads as empty. A key written into it would outrank every build setting.
- The two issues `v0.1.0` listed are **unchanged and untouched**: `tools/verify_faces_lab.py` still
  reports 7/16 assertions passing, and the debug WebSocket server (`net/BodyWsServer`) still binds
  `0.0.0.0:9008` with no authentication (documented in `SECURITY.md`).

## Before you install

Read **[DISCLAIMER.md](../../DISCLAIMER.md)** and
**[docs/acceptable-use.md](../acceptable-use.md)** first. On Android the app asks for accessibility,
overlay, notification access, SMS and call log, and it can complete a payment by tapping; on the Mac it
asks for accessibility, screen recording and notifications. The only guardrail on that path is Android's
`FLAG_SECURE`; the prompt-level checks on risky actions are instructions to the model, not enforcement.

**Speaking first is not an exception to that.** A bubble is text on your screen, and tapping a button on
it starts a turn **at the trust of the turn that wrote the bubble** — a bubble written by a turn that was
not allowed to send or pay cannot get you to send or pay by your tapping it either.

## Install from source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/kuafuai/andee
cd andee
git checkout v0.7.0

# one variant, by hand:
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug -PdefaultLang=en -PcodeflyingDomain=myapp.codeflying.app -PcodeflyingSsl=true

# or all ten files, the way the release was built:
bash ~/.workbuddy/skills/andee-release-ship/scripts/build_variants.sh
```

`-PcodeflyingKey=` and `-PgoogleVoiceKey=` are refused on purpose — a published package carries no
credential.

The design of the new behaviour, including what was deliberately **not** built, is in
**[docs/design/proactive-nudge.zh-CN.md](../design/proactive-nudge.zh-CN.md)** (Chinese).

**Upgrading:** `versionCode` moves `15 → 16`, so the Android package installs over v0.6.2 normally. The
Mac app's version string follows the same number. Existing memory, notebook, todo list and chat history
are untouched and are not migrated — nothing about their format changed. If you had picked the cream
look, note that it has been redrawn.
