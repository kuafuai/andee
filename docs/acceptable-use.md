# Acceptable Use Policy

> This is the second half of [DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md), and an extension of the
> "additional conditions" in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
> **Violating this policy takes you outside the licence.** Your right to use the software
> terminates automatically, and no disclaimer applies.
>
> Last updated: 2026-09-29

---

## 1. General

Use this software only for **lawful** purposes, and only on devices and accounts **you are entitled to operate**.

"You are entitled to operate" means the device is your own, or you have the device owner's explicit, informed permission. **"I was helping him" is not permission.**

## 2. Prohibited uses

The following are **prohibited without exception**. They are listed because they genuinely happen, not to paper over them:

**(a) Crime, and providing help to crime**

- Any criminal or unlawful activity.
- Telecom and network fraud, or **any form of assistance** to it — including transferring funds for others, money laundering ("跑分"), faking transaction flows, receiving and passing on proceeds, promotional referral, bulk account registration or unblocking, and creating or distributing fraud tooling.
  This **may constitute "the crime of aiding information network criminal activities" under Article 287-2 of the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China** — up to three years' imprisonment or detention, plus a fine. Note that in practice, **even without direct profit and even while claiming not to have known, a court may infer knowledge from objective conduct.**
- Operating another person's payment account, bank card, or settlement instrument on their behalf.

**(b) False information and reputational harm**

- Generating, publishing or spreading false information or rumours.
- Defaming, insulting or disparaging any person or organisation.
- Impersonating any person, institution or official identity.

**(c) Privacy**

- Recording, listening to, or reading another person's conversations, messages, contacts, call log, location or screen without their consent.
- Installing or running this software on a device that is not yours.

**(d) Circumventing security**

- Cracking, bypassing or evading any platform's risk controls, verification, limits or security measures.
- Bypassing payment verification, identity verification, or content moderation.
- Attacking, scanning or accessing any system, network or service without authorisation.

**(e) Other**

- Gambling, pornography, contraband, and any other prohibited transaction.
- Spam, harassment, or unsolicited bulk messaging and calling.
- Anything that would associate others with this project such that they could be mistaken for a party to the above.

## 3. On money — read this one separately

- **A human must be present for any financial action.** Do not let it complete a payment, transfer or order unattended.
- **Do not store a payment password in the vault and let it pay automatically.**
- It is **capable** of completing a transfer on its own — it taps by coordinates rather than going through a restricted payment API. **This project has no technical guardrail on that path.**
- **If money goes to the wrong place, or is lost to fraud, "the AI did it" is not a defence.** The legal consequence falls on the operator. (Chinese legal commentary makes the same point: an agent's autonomous "acting and deciding" may take the act outside the user's intent, but the consequence still lands on the user.)

## 4. On content published as you

- What it sends **counts as yours**. It carries your name, and your liability.
- In mainland China, spreading rumours may violate the Public Security Administration Punishments Law; in serious cases it may be a criminal offence.
- **Review before it publishes.** Do not let it speak to the outside world unattended.
- It can be steered by text on the screen (prompt injection) into saying things you did not ask for — see [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## 5. On other people's devices and accounts

- This policy permits **your own devices** and **your own accounts** only.
- LICENSE Part A.1(b) prohibits operating this software as a multi-tenant service that drives third parties' devices without written authorisation.
- If you need to manage other people's devices (an internal fleet deployment, for example), obtain written authorisation first.

## 6. Consequences

- **Licence termination**: violating this policy exceeds the scope granted by [LICENSE](../LICENSE), and your licence to use this software **terminates automatically**, with no notice from us.
- **Your liability**: all legal consequences are yours, not the authors' or contributors'.
- **What we reserve**: refusing support, closing the relevant issues, restricting or ending distribution, and reporting to the competent authorities where required.

## 7. Reporting misuse

If you find this software being used for any of the above, tell us through the channel in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). **Do not** post details in a public issue.

## 8. Notice

This document **is not legal advice.** It is a condition of use, not a legal opinion. Requirements differ between jurisdictions, and **satisfying yourself that your use is lawful is yours to do.**
